# The 3AM Neighbor

On our street, the power goes out at 3:00 every night. Exactly 3:00. Mom says it's the old wires. Dad says don't look outside. So of course, I looked. Through my window, I saw something taller than the power poles. Black fur. A grin full of teeth. It walked down the street, checking every house. Like counting. The next morning, there was a note on our door. Mom read it twice. Then she taped the window shut. Tonight, the lights went out at 2:59. One minute early. And something is knocking. Not on the door. On the roof.
